## 🚀 What is Angularize?

**Angularize.dev** is an AI-powered platform that generates complete, production-ready **Angular applications** from natural language prompts — instantly. Think of it like [Lovable](https://lovable.dev), but purpose-built from the ground up specifically for the **Angular ecosystem**.

You describe what you want to build. Our platform handles the rest — generating a full Angular application and deploying it, giving you a live URL you can visit immediately.

> Built by **Madhan Mohan** ([@Madhan-mohan14](https://github.com/Madhan-mohan14)) and **Mahbob**, as an independent product under active development.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

Angularize is powered by a **multi-agentic AI system** that takes your natural language prompt and orchestrates the full generation and deployment of an Angular application — end to end, without any manual steps.

You describe what you want. The system builds it, deploys it, and hands you back a **live URL** where your Angular app is running. From there, you choose what to do next.

---

## ✨ What You Get

Once Angularize processes your prompt, you receive:

- 🌐 **A live deployed URL** — your Angular app is instantly deployed and accessible in the browser
- 💻 **Full source code** — download the complete Angular project (components, services, routing, modules, TypeScript) and run it yourself with `npm install && ng serve`
- 🗄️ **Database integration** — connect your generated app to a database of your choice
- 📦 **Code download** — get the entire project as a zip, yours to modify and own

Everything generated follows Angular best practices — standalone components, reactive forms, RxJS, strong TypeScript typing — and is ready to extend.
